Neural Differential Equations

Concept Job

Problem: scientists watch a system change over time but don't know the rule driving that change

Domain: changing systems where time evolution is part of the model

Why it matters: time-stepping pins learning to continuous change, so tiny errors in the guessed rate repeat at every step and predictions drift wildly over many steps
