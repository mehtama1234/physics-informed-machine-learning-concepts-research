Domain Grounding

Standard

The page says where the concept matters in science or engineering and what quantity is being predicted or explained.

Strong Page

The domain, real quantity, and domain-specific failure test are visible.

Weak Page

The page describes a general model but never says what scientific object or quantity it serves.

Check

Look for domain guide links, worked examples, and concrete anchor pages.

Forbidden Shortcut

Do not say a method is useful in science without naming the scientific quantity and the decision that uses it.

Replacement Test

The page must name the domain object, the measured evidence, the hidden quantity, and the changed domain case that would reject the claim.
