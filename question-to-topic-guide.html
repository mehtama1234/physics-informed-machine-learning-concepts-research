Question To Topic Guide

Start With The Need

Use this page before choosing a method name. Say the ordinary scientific need first: what you can see, what you need next, and what would make the answer fail. Then open the first topic that carries that need.

The point is not to memorize labels. The point is to learn the route from a real shortage to a claim you can test. If the route cannot name the evidence, the missing quantity, and the first changed case, the explanation is not ready.

Everyday QuestionOpen This TopicWhy This Is The First StopWhat To Check Before Trusting It
I have many examples, but I do not have a short rule. Where do I start? Deep Learning lots of real examples but no equation to predict what comes next. The first move is to adjust many weights until the model maps familiar inputs to the right outputs. works on data it saw during training but breaks on new situations, new boundary, or bigger scale
I have a few measurements and a known equation. How do I make them work together? Physics-Informed Neural Networks sparse measurements, but the answer must follow known physical laws. The first move is to fit a neural network while also measuring how badly its output violates the known equation. penalty hides small errors near boundaries or sudden changes
My quantity changes across space and time. What language describes that change? Partial Differential Equations something changes everywhere at once—how do you describe that with math?. The first move is to write a local change rule that uses rates across space and time. if you ignore the constraints—like how fast something spreads or that nothing vanishes—your prediction breaks down far from the training data
I need the whole answer field for new inputs, not only one solved case. What carries that map? Operator Learning predict all values (temperature, pressure, flow) across a whole space at all times, for several starting conditions. The first move is to learn the map from problem input to solution, not only one solution at a time. the learned map looks reasonable but breaks physics underneath, or crashes when you try a different-shaped container
I need data, equations, units, and checks to live in one scientific claim. What is the broad home for that? Scientific Machine Learning teach a program both the data and the physics rules so it predicts correctly on cases it never saw. The first move is to combine learned prediction with scientific checks that name what the claim is allowed to mean. if the equation is wrong or doesn't exist, the program learns the data pattern but not the truth that works everywhere
My trusted solver is too slow to run for every design or control question. What is the first shortcut to study? Surrogate Modeling running the real simulator costs too much money or time to do hundreds of times. The first move is to train a cheaper stand-in for the expensive input-output behavior. confidently guesses wrong answers for situations outside its training set, with no warning it is guessing
I have a prediction, but I need to know when to believe it. What topic handles that? Uncertainty And Generalization how do you know when a prediction is trustworthy instead of a blind guess?. The first move is to separate fit on familiar examples from evidence on changed examples. model looks correct on familiar data while it silently gives nonsense in new terrain, new shape, new sensor, or new physical regime
The model is changing its settings. How do I know what it is being asked to improve? Optimization For Learning the program has millions of dials to turn and needs a way to know which turns make it better or worse. The first move is to change model settings to lower the written score. the number gets smaller while the program breaks the real physics, because the number never checked that rule
I need several possible fields, molecules, shapes, or scenarios, not one answer. Where do I start? Generative Modeling need several possible solutions, not just one best guess, to explore what could work. The first move is to learn how to sample new candidates that resemble the training family. a generated shape looks buildable but would collapse under load; a predicted fluid flow looks smooth and correct but loses mass where it shouldn't
My object has parts, neighbors, bonds, mesh points, or shape. Which topic keeps those relations visible? Graphs And Geometric Learning molecules, proteins, and meshes have a shape and connections that rows of numbers throw away. The first move is to let information move along the object connections instead of flattening the object into a plain row. if the graph misses a real bond or connection, or if parts of the shape rearrange and the model sees a stranger structure
A system changes over time, but the rate rule is partly unknown. What topic should I open? Neural Differential Equations scientists watch a system change over time but don't know the rule driving that change. The first move is to learn the missing rate rule and place it inside a time-evolution calculation. a 1% error in the learned rate, multiplied across hundreds of steps, sends predictions into completely wrong territory
I want a readable equation from measured variables. What topic is about that search? Symbolic Regression And Model Discovery you need an equation humans can read, not a that guesses numbers. The first move is to search for a readable equation that fits the data and survives a changed case. formula fits your lab data perfectly but breaks in the real world or uses nonsense variables
I want one field model to help across related PDE tasks. What should I read first? Foundation Models For PDEs scientists retrain from scratch for each equation, grid size, and number—duplicating work unnecessarily. The first move is to train one broad model to reuse structure across many related field-prediction tasks. model learns the common cases it trained on and breaks when facing rare physics events, unexpected shapes, or starting conditions it never encountered
Faraway parts of a field may affect the local answer. What topic explains that information route? Attention For Scientific Fields predicting what happens at one spot requires knowing what's happening far away, but checking everywhere is too slow. The first move is to let the model choose which parts of the field exchange information. when attention picks the wrong patches to look at, the model misses real physical effects traveling across the field

If Your Question Mentions Shape

If the question mentions a mesh, molecule, graph, boundary, surface, hole, neighborhood, or connected part, slow down before flattening the object into a plain row. Ask what relation carries the scientific quantity. Then open the shape guide and the topic row above that names the same relation.

Reader Test

A reader understands the first-principles route when they can start with one everyday question, choose one topic, and say why that topic is the first stop without using the method name as the reason. The answer should name the evidence in hand, the hidden thing needed, the mathematical move, and the changed case that could reject the claim.
