Discovering A Small Law From Motion

Scientific Job

Domain: a measured system changing over time

Question: Can data reveal a short rule for how the system moves?

First-Principles Story

Start with the decision a person is trying to make. The question is: Can data reveal a short rule for how the system moves? The observed evidence is measurements of position, speed, concentration, or another changing quantity. That evidence is useful, but it is not the whole answer. The hidden part is the rate rule that causes the next moment.

The method route is not a list of names. It is the ordered bridge from evidence to hidden quantity: symbolic regression -> neural differential equations -> optimization for learning. Each step has to earn its place. If a step does not add a rule, a structure, a cheaper calculation, or a trust check that the job needs, it should not be in the route.

This example teaches the field for a concrete reason: It separates prediction from explanation. A readable rule becomes valuable only when it survives a changed experiment. A reader should be able to retell it as a plain chain: what is known, what is missing, what mathematical object carries the missing part, what decision uses the answer, and what changed case would reject the claim.
