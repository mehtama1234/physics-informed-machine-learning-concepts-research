Physics-Informed Learning Flow

Purpose

Show how measured data and a known physical rule both push on the fitted field.

Flow

1. sparse measurements
2. known equation
3. fitted field
4. data check plus equation check
5. held-out sensor or trusted solve

Watch for: The equation check must be hard enough to catch mistakes between measured points.
