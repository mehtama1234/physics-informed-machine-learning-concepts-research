Model Discovery Flow

Purpose

Show how measurements can lead to a candidate rule rather than only a prediction.

Flow

1. measured motion
2. candidate variables
3. searched rule or learned rate
4. readable law
5. new experiment check

Watch for: A short law can be wrong if important variables were never measured.
