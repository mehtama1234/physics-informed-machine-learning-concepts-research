Fluids And Flow

Real Quantity

velocity, pressure, vorticity, drag, lift, or other flow quantities

Why This Domain Is Hard

small changes in shape, boundary, or regime can create large changes in the field

Common Question

Can we predict flow fields or forces quickly enough for design while still catching important failures?

Concepts That Matter

  • Operator Learning
  • Surrogate Modeling
  • Attention For Scientific Fields
  • Uncertainty And Generalization

How The Methods Enter

  • Name the shape and flow family.
  • Train on trusted simulated fields.
  • Check forces, boundary behavior, and difficult regimes rather than only visual similarity.

Failure Test

Hold out a new geometry or flow condition near the edge of the intended design range.
