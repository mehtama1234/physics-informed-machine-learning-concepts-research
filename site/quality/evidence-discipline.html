Evidence Discipline

Standard

The page separates transcript support from scientific proof.

Strong Page

Transcript evidence is shown as support that a concept appears, while validation claims require explicit tests.

Weak Page

The page treats a lecture mention as proof that a method works broadly.

Check

Look for transcript evidence, support type, and explicit evidence limits.

Forbidden Shortcut

Do not treat a source mention as proof that a method works on a new scientific case.

Replacement Test

Every source-backed claim must state what the transcript supports and what experiment, solve, or changed case would still be needed.
