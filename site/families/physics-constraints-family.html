Physics Constraints Family

Problem This Family Solves

You have some observations, but the answer must also obey a rule scientists already trust.

Where It Shows Up

heat flow, fluids, waves, elasticity, reaction systems, and other systems described by differential equations

Route Through The Ideas

1. Start with a quantity that changes across space or time.
2. Write the rule that says how the quantity is allowed to change.
3. Fit the unknown field to the measured values.
4. Also check whether the fitted field breaks the rule between measured values.
5. Test a changed boundary, changed source, or changed scale before trusting the result.

Concepts In This Family

  • Partial Differential Equations
  • Physics-Informed Neural Networks
  • Optimization For Learning
  • Uncertainty And Generalization

What The Math Buys

The equation turns empty space between measurements into a checkable demand. The model cannot claim success only by touching the measured points.

Failure Boundary

This family fails when the written equation is incomplete, the boundary information is wrong, or the training process avoids the hard regions where the rule matters most.

Reader Check

You understand this family when you can say the scientific job, the input family, the output quantity, the rule or structure being kept, and the changed case that would make the claim fail.
