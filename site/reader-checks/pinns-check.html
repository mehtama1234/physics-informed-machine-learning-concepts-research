PINNs Reader Check

Setup

A wall has only a few temperature sensors, but the heat equation is trusted.

Questions

  1. What is observed?
  2. What is hidden?
  3. Which physical rule checks the spaces between sensors?
  4. What error terms should the training score include?
  5. What changed case would make you doubt the claim?

Strong Answer Should Say

Observed: sensor values, starting or boundary values, and the heat equation. Hidden: the full temperature field. The equation residual checks unsensed locations. The score needs data error, equation error, and boundary or starting error. A changed boundary, source, or held-out sensor should test the claim.

Weak Answer Warning

A weak answer says only that the neural network fits data.

Acceptance Sentence

A reader passes only if they can say, in ordinary language, Observed: sensor values, starting or boundary values, and the heat equation. Hidden: the full temperature field. The equation residual checks unsensed locations. The score needs data error, equation error, and boundary or starting error. A changed boundary, source, or held-out sensor should test the claim.

First-Principles Scoring Rubric

CriterionPassFail
Observed evidence Names what is actually known before the method is chosen. Starts with the method name.
Hidden quantity Names the field, rule, answer, or use range still missing. Treats the prediction as if it were already known.
Mathematical move Explains what the math carries from evidence to missing quantity. Uses a label without saying what job the math does.
Changed-case rejection Names a changed case that could make the claim fail. Reports a score without a failure condition.
