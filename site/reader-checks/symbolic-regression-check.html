Symbolic Regression Reader Check

Setup

Measurements suggest there may be a short law behind a changing system.

Questions

  1. Which variables were measured?
  2. Which candidate ingredients were allowed?
  3. What does the selected formula claim?
  4. What missing variable could make the formula wrong?
  5. What changed experiment should test it?

Strong Answer Should Say

The answer lists measured variables, allowed operations or ingredients, and the formula's claim about the system. It names at least one missing variable or untested regime and demands a changed experiment before calling the formula useful.

Weak Answer Warning

A weak answer trusts a neat formula because it fits the original data.
