Proof Burden

Claim

A method name never proves a scientific claim; only a named test under a meaningful changed case can carry that burden.

Explanation

A transcript mention shows that a topic appears in the course. A training score shows that a model matched a written score. A scientific claim needs more: a domain, quantity, use range, and failure test.

Reader Takeaway

Every strong page should say what the transcript supports and what it does not prove.
