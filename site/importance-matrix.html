Attention For Scientific Fields

Plain end-to-end story. The starting problem is predicting what happens at one spot requires knowing what's happening far away, but checking everywhere is too slow. In everyday words, the evidence in hand is large fields where one location may depend on other locations. The missing answer is which distant parts matter for the local prediction. The first-principles move is let the model choose which parts of the field exchange information. This matters because lets the computer route information only through the connections it needs, trading speed for reach.

Shape and topology link. Let distant parts of a shape or field exchange information when local neighbors are not enough. Attention matters here when faraway structure changes the local answer. The first check is: Change window size, long-range links, boundary regions, or point order.

Climate, fluids, and fields: Route information across fields where one region can affect another. Check: Change storms, fronts, boundaries, and long-range interactions and inspect the field.
