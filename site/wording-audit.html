Wording Audit

This page turns the plain-language rule into a concrete review surface for authored explanatory prose. Hard-stop terms are blocked on content pages by validation. Review terms are not automatically wrong, but each hit should earn its place by naming evidence, quantity, domain, and failure test. Transcript excerpts are kept visible on source pages but excluded from this wording count.
