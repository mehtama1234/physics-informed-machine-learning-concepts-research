Read Training As A Contract

Question

What is the model actually being rewarded for?

Why This Comes Here

A model follows the score it is trained on. If that score omits the scientific burden, training can improve the wrong thing.

Plain Goal

Understand optimization as repeatedly changing a model to reduce a written error score, then checking whether that score matches the scientific job.

No-Jargon Explanation

This step asks the reader to slow down before reaching for a method name. World: the model has adjustable parts. Evidence: a score says which answers count as better or worse. Missing piece: the adjusted model that performs the needed job is unknown. The point is to make the missing piece visible before choosing any model. Mathematical move: update the adjustable parts to reduce the score. That move is only useful if it survives the stated rejection test. Reject it when: the score goes down while the boundary, equation, rare case, or decision quantity fails. If a reader cannot say this in ordinary language, they are not ready to trust the technical page that follows.
