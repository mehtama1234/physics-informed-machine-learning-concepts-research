Foundation Model

Everyday Meaning

one broad model trained across many related tasks

Problem It Names

training a new model for every scientific task can be expensive

Why It Matters

shared structure may reduce repeated training if the new task truly belongs to the learned family

What To Watch For

broad training is not proof that a new regime, boundary, or quantity is covered
