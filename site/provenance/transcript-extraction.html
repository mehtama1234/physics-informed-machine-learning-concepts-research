Purpose

Show how captions become local source material.

Process

  1. Use yt-dlp to download metadata, English captions, and automatic captions when provided.
  2. Store raw VTT caption files by playlist and video id.
  3. Clean VTT captions into plain text transcripts.
  4. Keep raw VTT and cleaned text so extraction can be inspected later.
