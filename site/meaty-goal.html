Meaty End-To-End Goal

Goal

Turn the Physics-Informed Machine Learning site from a structured first-pass atlas into a teaching-grade research package that explains the paper family from first principles.

Target Reader

A new reader who does not know the math, machine learning terms, benchmark language, causal language, optimization language, or systems language.

Done Means

  1. The reader can start from a plain scientific problem before seeing a method name.
  2. The reader can name the real quantity being predicted, explained, controlled, designed, or discovered.
  3. The reader can name the available evidence: measurements, equations, simulations, boundary information, geometry, prior cases, or transcript support.
  4. The reader can name what is hidden, missing, or unknown.
  5. The reader can explain why the mathematical move follows from that missing piece.
  6. The reader can translate the formula shape into everyday language.
  7. The reader can say which domain the idea belongs to and why solving it matters there.
  8. The reader can say what the method keeps, what it ignores, and where it fails.
  9. The reader can name the changed case that would reject an overclaim.
  10. The reader can connect the concept to nearby concepts, examples, diagrams, and source anchors.

Every Core Page Must Contain

  • A concrete domain story that starts from a real scientific job.
  • A first-principles derivation from observed evidence to hidden quantity to mathematical move.
  • A plain formula explanation that says what every term carries.
  • A worked example and a wrong-use example.
  • A failure boundary and a changed-case rejection test.
  • Transcript anchors that state what the source supports and what it does not prove.
  • Links to nearby concepts, diagrams, derivations, examples, and reader checks.

Acceptance Sentence

A reviewer should be able to fill this in from the page alone:

This concept exists because scientists need ___, but they only observe ___. The hidden thing is ___. The math does ___ because ___. It matters in ___ domain because ___. It fails when ___. I would test it by changing ___.

Coverage Audit

Open the per-concept meaty goal coverage audit to see which required page parts are present and which proof pages to inspect.

Not Done If

  • The page starts with a method name but does not explain the world problem first.
  • The page says a model learns patterns without naming the quantity, evidence, hidden part, and failure test.
  • The page uses broad confidence words instead of a changed-case test.
  • The page has transcript evidence but does not state what the evidence fails to prove.
  • The page cannot be retold by a new reader in ordinary language.

How To Use This Goal

Review one concept at a time. If the page cannot satisfy the acceptance sentence without relying on outside knowledge, keep editing that page. Passing validation only proves the site is structurally coherent; this goal defines the editorial finish line.
