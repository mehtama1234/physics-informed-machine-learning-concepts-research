Far Field Information Matters

Situation

A local part of a scientific field depends on distant regions, but carrying every interaction is expensive.

Best Starting Point

Attention for scientific fields

Why This Fits

Attention gives a way to route selected information across a field while making the routing choice testable.

Use If

  • nonlocal effects are plausible
  • the routing or window choice can be varied
  • the scientific quantity depends on distant context

Avoid If

  • attention is treated as explanation by itself
  • long-range effects are not stress tested
  • window choices are fixed without evidence

Evidence Needed

window-change tests, long-range interaction tests, boundary-stress cases, and error on the scientific quantity
