Discovering A Small Law From Motion

Scientific Job

Domain: a measured system changing over time

Question: Can data reveal a short rule for how the system moves?

Observed And Hidden

Observed: measurements of position, speed, concentration, or another changing quantity

Hidden: the rate rule that causes the next moment

Method Route

  • Symbolic Regression And Model Discovery
  • Neural Differential Equations
  • Optimization For Learning

Plain Steps

1. Measure the variables that might matter.
2. Estimate how they change from moment to moment.
3. Search for a short rule or learn the missing rate.
4. Test on a new experiment, not only the original trace.

Why This Example Teaches The Field

It separates prediction from explanation. A readable rule becomes valuable only when it survives a changed experiment.

Claim Boundary

The example supports a method only inside the named scientific job. A wider claim needs a new test, not stronger wording.
