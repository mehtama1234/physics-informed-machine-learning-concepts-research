Domain Guides

These pages ground the concepts in real scientific settings. Each guide names the quantity, the decision, the hidden part, and the changed case that can reject the claim.

Heat And Diffusion

What is happening between sensors, later in time, or under a changed boundary?

Quantitytemperature, concentration, or another quantity spreading through space
Decisiondecide whether the wall, chip, or sample will exceed a safe temperature
Hidden Partthe temperature at unsensed locations and later times
Changed-Case Testmove the heat source or change the boundary temperature and compare against held-out sensors or a trusted solve

Fluids And Flow

Can we predict flow fields or forces quickly enough for design while still catching important failures?

Quantityvelocity, pressure, vorticity, drag, lift, or other flow quantities
Decisionscreen designs and decide which cases deserve expensive solver runs
Hidden Partthe field and forces for a new shape near the edge of the design range
Changed-Case Testhold out a new geometry or flow regime and check drag, lift, boundary behavior, and vortices

Materials And Mechanics

Can a model predict how a material or structure responds under a new load or shape?

Quantitystress, strain, displacement, failure location, or material response
Decisiondecide whether the part is safe enough or needs a changed design
Hidden Partinternal stress and the local region where failure may begin
Changed-Case Testchange the load path, defect, mesh, or boundary and check stress near failure regions

Chemistry And Biology

Can learned structure help predict scientific behavior while respecting the object being studied?

Quantitymolecular property, reaction behavior, concentration, binding, or biological response
Decisionchoose which candidate molecules deserve synthesis, testing, or closer calculation
Hidden Partwhich structural relations control the property or response in the new molecule
Changed-Case Testtest on a new scaffold, rare atom type, changed assay, or biological condition outside the familiar set

Many PDE Tasks

Can one trained model reuse structure across many related scientific tasks?

Quantitysolution fields across many equations, grids, parameters, or boundary settings
Decisiondecide whether the broad model is a useful shortcut or whether a task-specific solve is still needed
Hidden Partwhich shared structure transfers to the new PDE case and which parts do not
Changed-Case Testwithhold a full equation family, boundary type, scale, or quantity and compare against trusted solves

Data-Rich Scientific Prediction

Can a learned predictor answer a new scientific case without pretending the examples cover every future use?

Quantitya measured or simulated quantity predicted from many prior examples
Decisiondecide whether the prediction is good enough for screening, measurement, or closer simulation
Hidden Partthe answer for a new case whose source, scale, or condition may differ from the old cases
Changed-Case Testhold out a new source, scale, sensor condition, or rare case and check the quantity used for decisions

Scientific Model Building

How should a learned model combine measurements and known rules for a named scientific job?

Quantitythe physical or scientific quantity the model is supposed to explain, predict, or support
Decisiondecide whether the combined learned-and-scientific model can support a claim, design, or experiment
Hidden Partthe missing relation or field value needed for the scientific question
Changed-Case Testchange the experiment, boundary, scale, or measured variable and verify the scientific target directly

Learned Time Dynamics

Can a model learn the missing rule for how a system changes while keeping time behavior testable?

Quantitythe future state, path, rate of change, or event time of a system
Decisiondecide whether the learned dynamics can be used for forecasting, control, or experiment planning
Hidden Partthe missing rate rule and the future path under a new starting condition or input
Changed-Case Teststart from a new condition, run longer than the training window, and check drift, stability, and conserved quantities

Training Score Design

What should the training process reward so the learned answer serves the scientific job?

Quantitythe scientific quantity encoded by the terms of the training score
Decisiondecide whether the loss is a faithful contract for the claim being made
Hidden Partwhether the trained model is good because the science is right or only because the written score is easy to lower
Changed-Case Testchange the weights, hold out hard regions, and check the physical or decision quantity directly
