Theme Index For The Whole Course

This page organizes the 137 handwritten chapters by the problem each group is trying to solve. Use it after Review Path when you want to know where a chapter belongs in the larger course.

If you are starting from an application area rather than a chapter range, use Domain Map.

Chapters 1-12: Why Fast Physical Simulation Is Hard

Use this arc for: the first mental model of the field. These chapters explain why physical solvers are valuable, why repeated solves are painful, and why learned shortcuts need physical checks.

Subthemes: PINN training, shock stability, multiscale coupling, fluid learning, heart modeling, differentiable physics, operator inference, ROM stability, adaptive optimization, and hybrid twins.

Anchor reads: Chapter 1, Chapter 3, Chapter 9, Chapter 12.

Chapters 13-24: Compression Must Respect Motion And Scale

Use this arc for: understanding why smaller models fail when the important feature moves, sharpens, or depends on hidden fine-scale structure.

Subthemes: grid-aware networks, sensor evidence, transport, moving fronts, nonlinear manifolds, multiscale closure, learned kernels, and material microstructure.

Anchor reads: Chapter 13, Chapter 18, Chapter 21, Chapter 24.

Chapters 25-47: Dynamics Has Geometry

Use this arc for: observables, modes, coordinates, manifolds, and the idea that nonlinear systems can look simpler after the right quantities are watched.

Subthemes: rays, complex analysis, Koopman thinking, symbolic discovery, interaction kernels, time-series memory, closure models, local charts, and state-space geometry.

Anchor reads: Chapter 25, Chapter 35, Chapter 42, Chapter 47.

Chapters 48-69: Learning Must Survive Action

Use this arc for: the moment when a model is used to control, optimize, design, or manufacture something. Here the learned model is pushed by decisions, not merely evaluated on old data.

Subthemes: physics-AI integration, differentiable control, safety, solver training, stochastic checks, manufacturing, topology decisions, melt pools, materials, and design loops.

Anchor reads: Chapter 48, Chapter 58, Chapter 61, Chapter 69.

Chapters 70-103: Operators, Sensors, And Large Data Workflows

Use this arc for: inverse problems, field-to-field maps, streaming simulation data, balanced truncation, neural operators, and reusable trained components.

Subthemes: inverse regularization, visualization pipelines, sensor placement, operator networks, multifidelity learning, biology, trustworthy bias, reduced control, compression, and pretrained PINNs.

Anchor reads: Chapter 70, Chapter 80, Chapter 91, Chapter 102.

Chapters 104-123: Discovery, Turbulence, And Scientific Representation

Use this arc for: how data reveals hidden rules, coherent structures, equations, latent variables, materials behavior, plasma structure, and state estimation under moving dynamics.

Subthemes: reasoning-guided learning, flow surrogates, turbulent structures, weak-form discovery, latent spaces, materials foundation potentials, plasma particles, and moving-state sensors.

Anchor reads: Chapter 104, Chapter 113, Chapter 122, Chapter 123.

Chapters 124-137: Modern Trust Problems

Use this arc for: the most recent course themes: scientific latent spaces, online PDE control, geometry optimization, plasma collisions, AI simulation, foundation-model definitions, assimilation, uncertainty, invariant manifolds, and generative subsurface inference.

Subthemes: equation discovery, tokenization, particle methods, foundation models, data assimilation, UQ, SSMs, topology/design, generative priors, posterior sampling, and monitoring plans.

Anchor reads: Chapter 124, Chapter 132, Chapter 135, Chapter 137.
