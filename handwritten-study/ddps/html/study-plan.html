Study Plan For Learning The Area

This plan turns the handwritten DDPS course into a sequence. Do not try to read 137 chapters at once. Move through the support pages first, then read chapter arcs with a specific output after each block.

orient
translate terms
read chapter arcs
explain and test

Session 1: Build The Mental Floor

Read: From Zero, Big Picture, Examples.

Output: write two sentences explaining why scientific machine learning exists without using method names.

Skip for now: formulas, acronyms, and individual chapter details.

Session 2: Learn The Vocabulary Without Worshiping It

Read: Plain Words, Math Map, Common Mistakes.

Output: pick five terms and write: "This exists because blank is hard."

Skip for now: pretending every term is a separate field. Most terms are different answers to the same burden.

Session 3: Choose A Path

Read: Method Map, Domain Map, Theme Index, Prerequisite Map, Cheat Sheet.

Output: choose one method family and one domain. Write why they match.

Skip for now: reading every anchor chapter. Choose one route first.

Session 4: Read The First Arc

Read: Chapter 1, Chapter 3, Chapter 9, Chapter 12.

Output: explain why speed alone is not enough. Name the physical rule or behavior that can break.

Skip for now: chapters that only make sense after you know your chosen route.

Session 5: Read Your Chosen Arc

Read: use Theme Index to choose one chapter range. Read the anchor chapters first, then fill in nearby chapters if the idea matters to your goal.

Output: write the arc's burden: "This arc is about blank, and it fails first when blank."

Skip for now: unrelated arcs unless they answer a question you already have.

Session 6: Practice Explaining

Read: Self Check, then revisit weak pages.

Output: answer the ten prompts out loud without reading the expected answers first.

Skip for now: new chapters. Repair the explanation before adding more content.

Session 7: Finish The Big Story

Read: Capstone.

Output: write the whole course in one paragraph and one rejection sentence.

Skip for now: any claim that sounds impressive but does not say what would make it fail.

Session 8: Use It On New Work

Read: Paper Reader.

Output: take one new paper, chapter, or abstract and fill the worksheet: claim, burden, evidence, physical rule, changed case, uncertainty, decision, verdict.

Skip for now: believing the paper's own adjectives before you can write the plain version.

The Minimum Useful Path

If you only have one sitting, read From Zero, Method Map, Self Check, and Capstone. Then read one chapter that matches your domain or method interest.
