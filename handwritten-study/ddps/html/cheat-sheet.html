The Four Questions

What hurts?Too slow, too hidden, too uncertain, too high-dimensional, too unstable, or too expensive to repeat.
What evidence exists?Equation, simulation, sensor, image, trajectory, prior, geometry, material data, or expert rule.
What does learning carry?Loss, residual, basis, operator, manifold, latent space, posterior, controller, or generator.
What breaks it?Changed geometry, grid, boundary, regime, noise, time horizon, prior, sensor layout, or physical constraint.

Method In One Line

Method Family Plain Job Do Not Trust Until
PINNMake a neural field obey data and equations.Every important loss term matters during training.
ROMReplace a huge solver with a small stand-in.The small model preserves the behavior that controls the decision.
ManifoldFind the smaller curved state space where motion lives.The manifold is observed, attracting, and tested outside the fitting case.
OperatorLearn a map from whole fields to whole fields.It survives changed grids, geometries, boundaries, and regimes.
Data assimilationUpdate a model when observations arrive.The data can identify the hidden state and the prior is not misleading.
UQAttach the right doubt to a prediction.The uncertainty sources match the decision risk.
Generative modelCreate many plausible worlds or futures.The samples are physically valid and decision-relevant.
Foundation modelReuse learned structure across many tasks.Transfer is tested across real physical changes.
