Plain-Language Glossary

These terms are translated by the job they do. Each page states the everyday meaning, the real problem, why the term matters, and what to watch for.

Field

a value spread across space or time, like temperature across a room

Boundary Condition

what is known at the edge of the problem

Residual

the leftover rule-breaking after a proposed answer is checked

Loss

the score the training process tries to lower

Operator

a machine that takes one whole function or field and returns another

Surrogate

a faster stand-in for something slower

Generalization

whether a model still works on a new case

Uncertainty

a warning about how much the answer may be wrong

Symbolic Regression

searching for a short formula that fits measured behavior

Foundation Model

one broad model trained across many related tasks
